# ziviSynthi AKS 3.9.6 · Pre-release

**For the M‑VAVE SMK‑37 Pro.** USB identity `SMK-37 Pro_99500`.

ziviSynthi AKS is an independent digital interpretation of the EMS Synthi AKS. It brings pin-matrix patching and a KS-inspired keyboard sequencer to the SMK‑37 Pro and to Web Studio.

- Redesigned boot screen with a clearer wordmark and an animated 16 × 16 patch matrix.
- English-first website with Portuguese navigation, historical references and a clearer route into the browser synth and installer.
- 128 instrument patches, four USB effects, three oscillators, resonant filtering, ring modulation, envelope shaping and digital spring-style reverb.
- Web Studio offers local playback and patch editing, plus bidirectional control of a compatible Pro over USB MIDI. Local playback is also available in the downloadable HTML.

The firmware image and updater have passed build-time checks and host simulations. Flashing, analog output and Bluetooth on a physical unit remain to be verified for this pre-release. The DSP and live-edit protocol are unchanged from 3.9.5.

**Try first.** Open [Web Studio](https://vicvalentim.github.io/ziviSynthi-AKS/studio/#try) to play without hardware. Before flashing, read the [installation guide](https://vicvalentim.github.io/ziviSynthi-AKS/docs/usage/INSTALL.en.html) and back up patches you want to keep. The installer verifies the model and the approved firmware hash.

## Português

Pré-release para o **M‑VAVE SMK‑37 Pro**, com identidade USB `SMK-37 Pro_99500`. A abertura agora tem uma matriz 16 × 16 animada e tipografia mais legível. O site começa em inglês e oferece navegação em português, referências sobre o EMS Synthi AKS original, sintetizador no navegador, editor de patches e instalador.
